# Peer review 0011 - graph freeze transfer and contract-normalized Slot comparison

Reviewed `origin/patch_v2` at `4b214e2`, including `PV2_0044` and
`PV2_0046`. Absolute scores remain outside our fixed HDF5 contract and are not
pooled with SW results.

## New peer result

The peer ported SW0072's trained-graph freeze intervention, selecting its strong
seed1 graph and retraining seeds0/2 downstream. On its fixed 300-image
validation slice at T1024/settle512, the three shared-graph seeds score
`.7250/.7188/.4953` (FG-ARI/foreground IoU/matched-object IoU). This exceeds
the peer's single official Slot checkpoint reference
`.6195/.1241/.0920` on all three metrics. As in SW0072, the seeds are not fully
independent because they share the selected graph; the very small variance is
therefore not evidence that graph learning itself became seed-stable.

The transfer is reciprocal evidence for the same mechanism: graph variation is
a major source of weak seeds in both branches, and fixing a successful learned
graph lets the downstream Kuramoto-to-spike path retain useful grouping.
SW0072 already implements this intervention on our contract, so no code port is
needed.

## Why the Slot conclusions differ

The peer evaluates `clevr_with_masks` at 240x320, converted to a 16x16 grid by
majority vote over 15x20-pixel patches. Its target foreground prevalence is
about `.1146`. Our HDF5 masks are 128x128, converted to the same grid from
8x8-pixel patches, with foreground prevalence around `.199-.217`.

The identical official Slot checkpoint scores FG-ARI `.6195` under the peer
contract and `.8946` under ours. The peer Slot audit also transfers a checkpoint
trained on the original CLEVR render to a different `clevr_with_masks` render,
uses one checkpoint rather than a matched three-seed mean, and evaluates the
seven-slot-trained model with 11 inference slots. Thus the peer's statement that
its model exceeds Slot is correct for its registered reference, but it does not
establish superiority to a Slot model trained on the same data.

Normalized only as a contract diagnostic, peer `PV2_0044` reaches `1.17x` its
Slot FG-ARI while SW0072 reaches `0.87x` ours. These ratios explain the different
headline conclusions; they do not make the raw model scores cross-contract
comparable.

## Incorporation decision

1. Preserve the HDF5 contract and SW0072 shared-graph result; do not change
   targets to reproduce the peer's lower Slot reference.
2. Keep the matched-data, three-seed Slot run as the required final comparator.
3. Treat joint feature learning with reconstruction as the remaining route to
   raise the graph/feature ceiling. SW0084 already tests this with the encoder,
   graph, and downstream core trainable together; its trained checkpoints are
   queued for evaluation when an allowed GPU is idle.
4. Report any shared-graph three-seed result with that dependency explicit.
