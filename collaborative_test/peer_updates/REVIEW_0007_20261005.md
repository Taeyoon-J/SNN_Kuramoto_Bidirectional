# Peer review 0007 - what feature property matters

Reviewed peer commit `6677510` (`PV2_0035-36`). The oracle-guided sweep keeps
the real encoder's between-object distance fixed and reduces only within-object
spread. FG-ARI rises from `.7239` at real spread `.465` to `.8935` at zero
spread; foreground IoU reaches `.9340` and matched-object IoU `.6721`.
Increasing the between-object margin accounts for the remaining gain to
FG-ARI `.9837`.

The masks used to construct the sweep make these diagnostics only. They cannot
be used for training or reported as model scores. The transferable conclusion
is narrower: label-free within-object feature consistency is the highest-value
feature target, followed by between-object margin.

SW0062 tested the suggested off-the-shelf DINO route and found a CLEVR-domain
mismatch: DINO/PCA features had worse within-object consistency than native
gamma. SW0063 then tested an edge-aware consistency transform in the native
basis. It improved FG-ARI but not both IoU endpoints. The next action is the
SW0064 activation-flow probe rather than further feature variants.
