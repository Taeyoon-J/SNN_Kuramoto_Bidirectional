# Peer review 0010 - spike timing audit and encoder-capacity stop

Reviewed `origin/patch_v2` at `ade4a46` after SW0076 completed. The new peer
evidence is a protocol audit on BIM6 seed1, not a directly pooled score.

## Findings

- The scored signal is a gated spike: a binary membrane-threshold crossing
  multiplied by the continuous gamma gate.
- Recomputing affinity from binary crossings alone correlates `0.9822` with the
  scored gated-spike affinity. Only `0.5%` of units are always active in the
  settled window and binary activity has per-unit standard deviation `0.4041`.
  Under that configuration, spike timing rather than gate amplitude carries the
  grouping structure.
- The spatial kernel changes peer FG-ARI from `0.6887` to `0.7059`. In the
  separate spectral control, spatial-only is `0.3902` and spike affinity is
  `0.6068`, so the spike signal is not explained by geometry alone.
- Increasing encoder capacity after adding reconstruction does not help: depth2
  diverges (`23.56` to `86.69`, zero groups) and depth3 reaches FG-ARI `0.3910`
  with only `1.93` predicted objects versus `6.20` true. Depth1 reconstruction1
  remains the peer end-to-end lead at `0.6779`.

## Relevance to this branch

The peer non-saturation result does not invalidate our earlier saturation
diagnostic because membrane thresholds and readout windows differ. It does show
that the conclusion is configuration-specific and supplies a cheap classifier
control: compare our gated component-spike affinity with affinity computed from
the binary nonzero crossing pattern on the same frozen SW0072 checkpoint.

SW0073-SW0076 already show that further encoder tuning and local preservation
anchors do not improve all three metrics. The peer capacity failure reinforces
the decision to stop increasing encoder depth. The next classifier pilot will
therefore include a binary-only spike control and a label-free RGB-border
foreground rule, while keeping SW0072's learned graph and downstream dynamics
frozen. No peer score is combined with our aligned HDF5 results.
