# Peer review 0005 - low-LR transfer and phase-to-spike ceiling

Reviewed 2026-10-04 from `origin/patch_v2` commit `b10fe87`. The peer
renderer, split, foreground prevalence, and evaluation protocol remain
different from the fixed HDF5-aligned contract used by `patch_v2_sw`, so the
absolute scores below are not pooled with our results.

## New peer result

The peer ported our low-learning-rate/early-stop recipe. On its three regular
seeds, `lr=3e-4, epoch=25` reduced the FG-ARI range from `.066` to `.035`, but
the mean fell from `.7059` to `.6657`; foreground IoU also worsened. It did not
repair peer seeds 3/4/5. Peer seed 5 remained effectively silent and was
previously revived by an inference-time membrane-threshold change, so its
failure is downstream threshold calibration rather than the phase-side seed
collapse observed in our SW0049/SW0052 seed 2.

The peer also reports long-window FG-ARI `.7255` from the phase diagnostic and
`.7059` from the spike readout. Its spike path therefore loses about `.0196`
relative to theta under connected components. This is evidence that
phase-to-spike transduction remains a bottleneck on the peer contract; it is
not a mathematical upper bound on spike performance.

## Incorporation decision

1. Do not copy the peer low-LR result as evidence against our selected recipe.
   The same recipe repaired our phase-side collapsed seed 2, while the peer's
   dead seed has a different failure mechanism.
2. Keep SW0055 labeled as a data-scale control. It tests unique-scene diversity
   at matched exposure and cannot establish a model or mechanism improvement.
3. Continue SW0058 on the original SW0053 1,000-scene training tensor. Its only
   intended training change is removing the base phase-PLV contribution while
   retaining the component-spike synchrony term. This directly tests whether
   the objective can reduce a phase-to-spike bypass without changing data.
4. Evaluate both fixed spike and membrane readouts and preserve phase only as a
   diagnostic. If SW0058 improves phase but not spike/membrane masks, reject it
   as a transduction improvement.
5. Record per-seed phase, membrane, and spike separation plus event-rate and
   membrane-range diagnostics. A silent-spike failure should trigger threshold
   calibration analysis; a low phase separation should trigger graph/loss
   analysis. Do not apply one remedy to both failure shapes.

No peer code or checkpoint is merged by this review. The actionable transfer
is the failure-shape distinction and the requirement that an actual model/loss
experiment show improvement after theta, under our unchanged evaluation data.
