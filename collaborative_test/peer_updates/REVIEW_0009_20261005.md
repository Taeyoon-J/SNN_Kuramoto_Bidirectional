# Peer review 0009 - reconstruction-held end-to-end features

Reviewed `origin/patch_v2:b9c31ed` after SW0072. The commit adds the
PV2_0039-41 result record; its relevant reconstruction primitives were already
present in our working loss module, so no broad branch merge is needed.

PV2_0039 establishes a useful mechanism on the peer protocol. PLV-only joint
training collapses the encoder, while phase-slot RGB reconstruction at weight
`1.0` changes the peer seed0 result from `.6639/.6255/.4806` to
`.6779/.6651/.4692`. Weight `5.0` collapses performance. The gain is therefore
a tradeoff rather than an all-metric result, and its validation set differs
from our fixed HDF5 contract. The generated gamma scale is also about ten times
smaller than the frozen gamma scale.

We incorporate the mechanism through SW0073 rather than pooling its numbers.
SW0073 keeps the causally successful SW0072 trained graph bitwise frozen, uses
our verified HDF5-aligned RGB/gamma ordering, and tests coarse reconstruction
weights `0.3` and `1.0` against the actual SW0072 seed1 baseline. GPU2/3 are
left to Taeyoon's uncommitted PV2_0041 arms (`weight=2` and `weight=1,
slots=10`); our work remains on GPUs0/1.
