is a tie, not a win; the real result is the two IoUs.

Their own records say as much. SW0135 calls the earlier single-seed native32
observation (`.817187/.760535/.641405`, SW0114, frozen encoder and graph) "not a
native32 Slot victory", and SW0118's `.838763` against own-data Slot `.828048` is

## One mechanism worth having

Slot Attention's foreground IoU is **0.2044 on their contract and 0.1184 on ours**.
Two resolutions, two label rules, two training budgets, and it still over-predicts
foreground — 72% of patches here, and a predicted background fraction of 0.15
against a target of 0.88. That is a property of the model, not of either contract,
and it is where both branches' advantage actually comes from.

## What was not ported, and why

- **native32 itself.** Tempting, since their FG-ARI tie and our 0.7250 are both
  short, and a finer grid changes what a patch is. But our contract v1 is fixed and
  changing it raises the version and requires re-evaluating both models; and their
  own native32 FG-ARI did not beat Slot. Not a fix for condition 1.
- **SW0124 temporal-prototype readout**: `.751922/.397001/.552672` against their
  unchanged `.776348/.577070/.601537`. Lower on all three, paired CI negative.
- **SW0123 adaptive temporal assignment**: `.038607/.048265/.006983`. Failed.
- **SW0121 competitive assignment**: `.824101/.717444/.653268`, below their frozen
  incumbent on all three, paired FG CIs crossing zero.
- **SW0119 density-target removal** (+0.000412, CI crosses zero) and **SW0120
  flat-trace gradient guard** (FG did not improve). Neither passed their gate.
- **SW0132** terminated on a row-scramble guard; **SW0133** phase_live
  `.745245/.599191/.585315` exceeds its two controls in one seed but is below their
  own incumbent.

So of 25 records, none carries a positive, reproduced, promotable result to port.
Their incumbent is still SW0097.

## Worth watching
