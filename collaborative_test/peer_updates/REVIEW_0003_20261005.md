# Peer review — `origin/patch_v2_sw` at `1724f92`

38 commits since `5483920`. Their numbers are on their own split, render and
foreground prevalence (HDF5 IDs 1320-1639), so **nothing here is pooled with ours
numerically** -- only mechanisms transfer.

## Their new lead: SW0072, 3-seed mean FG-ARI 0.7814

| | FG-ARI | foreground IoU | matched-object IoU |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| seed 0 | **0.8171** | 0.7301 | 0.6439 |
| seed 1 | 0.7541 | 0.3770 | 0.5709 |
| seed 2 | 0.7730 | 0.3270 | 0.6355 |
| **mean** | **0.7814** | 0.4780 | 0.6167 |

up `+0.1040 / +0.0426 / +0.1369` on SW0070. The method: **load the trained seed-0
graph, freeze it, and retrain seeds 1 and 2 downstream.** Graph tensors stay
bitwise equal to the source.

### They localised the cause, then made it causal

- **SW0064**: identical gamma diverges most at the **graph-to-Kuramoto
  conversion** -- late PLV object margin 0.8167 for seed 0 against 0.5167 for
  seed 2, while dendrite-to-membrane propagation is nearly lossless and
  membrane-to-spike a smaller secondary loss.
- **SW0065**: swapping seed 0's trained **graph generator** into seed 2 moves
  their 32-image result from `.3895/.2003/.1822` to `.7036/.3081/.5376`. Swapping
  the drive or the Kuramoto parameters does not improve all three.
- **SW0066**: fixed graph *initialisation* helps both weak seeds but seed 1's
  foreground IoU falls 0.0285, so their per-seed gate fails.
- **SW0070**: graph-init0 as a stable-core baseline, `.6774/.4354/.4799`.

### Worth porting, and on an axis we have not tested

Our graph work measured graph **quality** and found it spent: a ground-truth
coupling graph was worth only +0.06 (`PV2_0005`). This is graph **stability
across seeds**, which we never varied. Our own seed problem is the same size --
seed 0 chronically weak, and two of six seeds unusable (`PV2_0012`) -- and we
localised it to membrane threshold saturation (`PV2_0023`) where they localised it
to the graph. Both can hold at once: the graph diverges, and the consequence
shows up as a membrane distribution that the fixed threshold cannot read.

`--freeze-graph` is implemented here for that, to be used with `--init-from`.

### One caveat to carry into any report

With seed 0's graph frozen into seeds 1 and 2, **the three seeds share a
component**, so the variance the graph contributes is removed by construction and
the resulting mean is not three independent runs. It is a strong mechanism result;
as a 3-seed protocol claim it needs that stated. Their own Slot reference is
FG-ARI 0.8946, so 0.7814 remains below it on ARI.

## They independently reproduced three of our findings

- **SW0062**: acted on our `084a2ef`/`96d74d6` (feature separation is the
  bottleneck), tried frozen DINOv2-small with an 8D PCA basis, and stopped it
  after a pilot because its **CLEVR within-object consistency was worse than
  native gamma**. That is our `PV2_0037` conclusion reached separately.
- **SW0068/SW0069**: joint encoder training lowers their metrics; "the failure is
  harmful core co-adaptation, not wholesale feature collapse" and "the binding
  loss remains misaligned as an encoder objective". That is our `PV2_0039`
  finding -- PLV alone degrades the features -- from a different direction.
- **SW0061**: substituting RGB/chroma/edge patch gamma into a fixed checkpoint
  fell to `.3080/.3831/.1909`, showing "the trained core does not accept an
  arbitrary feature basis". Consistent with our end-to-end gamma, whose range was
  about ten times smaller than the frozen tensor and which only worked with the
  core trained on it.

## Where we disagree, and it is measured on both sides

- **Spike saturation.** Their SW_0034 found the binary threshold "constant 1
  after settle" with variation gate-driven. On our model only **0.5%** of units
  are always on and the binary part's per-unit std is 0.40, and the binary-only
  affinity reproduces the scored affinity at **r = 0.982** (`PV2_0043`). Our
  membrane-threshold work and 1024-step readout act on exactly that, so the same
  architecture saturates or does not depending on configuration.
- **Label-free smoothing.** Their SW0063 improved FG-ARI by +0.0293 with
  label-free smoothing on the native basis. Ours *lowered* it, 0.7239 to 0.6965
  (`PV2_0037`), with the measured within-object spread improving at the same time.
  Different implementations; theirs is worth reading in detail.

## Not ported

- **SW0067** (fixed foreground-area calibration): their 3-seed mean fell
  `.6187/.4785/.4369` to `.5120/.4917/.3464` with count MAE worsening 1.927 to
  2.490. We reached the same conclusion from the other side -- pinning predicted
  foreground to the target fraction did not help here either.
- **SW0071** (spike-freeze readout): `+0.0220 / 0 / -0.0333`; they stop the
  direction. Our `PV2_0015` frozen-path work agrees that whole-component
  repartitioning does not pay.
