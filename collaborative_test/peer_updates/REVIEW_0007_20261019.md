## 1. They now beat Slot Attention on all three — on a different leaderboard

| | FG-ARI | Foreground IoU | Object IoU |

Their own phrasing is that all three means strictly exceed Slot, and they re-derived 2,880
raw masks independently to check it. This is a real result and it is more than we have: we
win two of three and lose FG-ARI.

**Three conditions, all of which they state themselves, keep it off our board:**

1. **It is a mask-supervised lineage.** Traced: `SW_0155` declares the separate supervised
   leaderboard under the user's explicit authorisation for TRAIN instance masks →
   `SW_0221` mask-supervised encoder-frequency pretraining → `SW_0222` teacher denoising from
   frozen TRAIN teachers → `SW_0223` joint projection → `SW_0227` evaluates that checkpoint.
   `SW_0227` says it plainly: *mask-supervised lineage이며 … 동일 supervision/cost의
   unsupervised 비교가 아니다.* **Our contract admits no ground truth in training or
   selection**, so this is not a number we can stand beside ours.
2. **Repeatedly-used validation, not a test read.** *반복 사용한 validation으로, untouched
   test 성능이라고 부르지 않는다.* Our 0.7603 is a single test read.
3. **A different contract.** native64, their 320 images, modal patch GT. Their Slot reference
   is .786902 against our .8102 — different preprocessing, grid and split, so the two
   columns are not comparable and must not be placed in one table.

Their Slot reference is, like ours, **one published checkpoint**, and they say so. One thing
checked and cleared: their baseline states *images were scaled to [-1,1] without an extra
crop*, so the misalignment `PV2_0090` found on our side is **not** present on theirs. The
item sent back in `REVIEW_0006` is answered.

## 3. The finding that converged on both branches

**Merge alone costs matched-object IoU.**

| | merge kind | Object IoU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PV2_0107b` (ours, 3 seeds) | label-free, affinity agglomeration | .6588 → **.6303** |
| `SW_0234` (theirs, 3 seeds) | **GT-prescribed** | .674313 → **.667059** |

Ours was found yesterday with a label-free merger and could have been blamed on the
affinity picking wrong pairs. Theirs is an *oracle* merge — the pairs are chosen with the
truth — and it still loses Object IoU. **So the loss is not a failure of pair selection.**
Merging pieces of one object raises FG-ARI and, where the pieces were separately matched to
separate true objects, destroys the match. They reach the same instruction we did:
*FG만 보고 단순 merge 방향을 밀어붙이지 않는다.*

`SW_0214` is the learned version of that stage, and it failed outright: FG-ARI
.788414 → .720607 (−.068, CI excludes 0), Object IoU .674335 → .580461 (−.042). The relation
loss trained cleanly, .693147 → .144341, and the masks got worse — the familiar shape here.

## 4. The design constraint this hands our coverage stage

This is the part that changes our plan.

`SW_0207` is their **label-free-at-inference** foreground stage — a trained classifier for
foreground support, *prediction uses no GT or object count*. It is **closed, not adopted**:

| fixed16, seed 2 | FG-ARI | Foreground IoU | Object IoU |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| original | .827671 | .444883 | .689239 |
| learned foreground | .773808 | **.616158** | .659627 |

Foreground IoU **+.171275** — and FG-ARI −.053863 with Object IoU down too, because
*instances fragment more*. Closed without a threshold or LR rescue.

Set that against `SW_0234`'s oracle recovery, which improves **all three** (+.0248 / +.0359 /
+.0877). The two differ in exactly one respect, and they describe it: the oracle
*전경 복구는 … 완전히 누락된 물체에는 새 그룹도 만든다* — it creates **new groups for objects
that were missed entirely** — and it *서로 다른 물체를 이미 합친 그룹은 분리하지 않는다*.
`SW_0207` instead widened the support of groups that already existed.

**So a coverage stage that widens existing groups buys foreground IoU and loses the other
two; a coverage stage that adds missed objects as new groups gains all three.** That is a
constraint on our design, derived from two of their records, before we write a line of it.

## 5. What we take, and what we do not

**Not ported, numerically: everything.** SW0227 is mask-supervised, on their contract, on a
reused validation split. Nothing in it can enter our column.

**Taken as mechanism:**

1. The oracle merge still losing Object IoU — this retires the hypothesis behind our planned
   `PV2_0111` linkage sweep. We were going to test complete linkage against mean linkage on
   the belief that the pairs were wrong. Their result says the pairs can be *right* and the
   column still falls. `PV2_0111` as scoped is no longer worth the compute.
2. The coverage constraint in §4: **new groups for missed objects, never wider support for
   existing ones.**
3. `SW_0207` and `SW_0214` as registered negative prior art: a learned merger and a learned
   foreground-support head have both been tried on that side and both closed.

**Consequence for our plan.** Coverage moves ahead of linkage, and it is specified before it
is built. `PV2_0111` is withdrawn in its current form and renumbered: the next test is a
label-free coverage stage that only instantiates groups for unclaimed regions.
