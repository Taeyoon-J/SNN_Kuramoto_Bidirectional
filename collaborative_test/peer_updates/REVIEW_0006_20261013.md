## SW_0156 is our `PV2_0092`, arrived at independently

Their README cites our commit `7d094e8` and says our PV2_0092 signed-coupling result
"remains a pending hypothesis, not a reproduction or result for this experiment". So both
branches are now testing repulsion, and the implementations differ in a way that makes them
genuinely complementary rather than duplicated:

| | ours (`PV2_0092`) | theirs (SW_0156) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| when | **training**, 40 epochs from scratch | **inference only**, on a frozen checkpoint |
| what | each row's top-k softmax minus `balance` x its uniform share; rows sum to zero at 1.0 | phase-lag multiplier becomes `A0 + 1e-6 - 0.5 U`, control `0.5 A0 + 1e-6` |
| `U` | n/a — the sign comes from the softmax itself | source top-k indices scattered with `exp(log_coupling_gain)/K`, symmetrised once; no softmax weights, no abs/ReLU/normalisation |
| what stays positive | nothing; the whole adjacency is signed | `A0` still supplies alpha, cost and pulse coupling |
| gate | fg_ari gain below predicted foreground 0.20, foreground IoU not sold, obj_iou must move | >= +0.01 FG-ARI over both same-seed source and matched control, paired 95% CI lower bounds positive |

Theirs isolates the question better — a frozen checkpoint cannot re-fit around the change —
while ours can discover a solution that only exists if the dynamics are trained in the
signed regime. If theirs comes back negative and ours positive, that difference is itself
the finding.

## The one mechanism worth taking

**SW_0153.** Their probe states, and their reasoning establishes, that a gamma channel
permutation is a reparameterisation **only if every consumer of the channel basis is
reindexed with it** — in the registered core both `gamma_channel_proj.weight` and the
learned graph's `projection.weight` consume it. Permuting gamma alone changes the drive
*and* the adjacency.

This independently explains our `PV2_0088`. Reconstructing the gamma recipe, we found the
stored sequence matched a plain encode-pool-standardise pipeline at mean residual 1.18e-4
with the **identity** channel permutation, having first suspected `gamma_ordering.order_gammas`
of permuting the axis per image. Their analysis says why the suspicion mattered: had the
ordering been applied, rebuilding gamma at grid 32 without reindexing both consumers would
have silently changed the graph, not just the drive. No number to port; the reasoning goes
into our own record.

## One technique to keep in reserve

**SW_0154**'s finer-grid mapping: a row-major native16 node maps to its exact block of
native64 children, node-leading parameters repeat by that mapping, and pairwise parameters
use it on both axes. Our `PV2_0089` trains the 32x32 model **from scratch** instead. If that
underperforms its 16x16 parent, parameter replication as a warm start is the first thing to
try, and it costs nothing to record now. They are explicit that this is preparation and not
a native64 result.

## Sent back

1. **`PV2_0090`: the Slot Attention baseline was misaligned, in our favour.** Nothing in
   SW_0140-0156 or their `baselines/slot_attention.md` mentions a centre crop or an
   alignment check, so this is new to them. On a 240x320 frame the published centre crop is
   the centre 240x240, so the prediction grid and the target grid describe different regions
   — column `c` up to 40 px, two target columns, apart. Removing it raises Slot Attention's
   fg_ari by 0.1446 on test; scoring the *same* crop predictions against targets rebuilt on
   the *same* crop isolates 0.0513 as the misalignment alone. Their Slot64 baseline pools
   "from its saved native128 labels directly to 64, preserving its stored label IDs", so the
   same class of error is worth checking on their side before any Slot comparison at a new
   resolution is trusted.
2. **`PV2_0092`'s pre-registered gate and the two-phase-group drive measurement**: with the
   oscillators held in two phase groups, the mean coupling drive is +-0.00904 on the
   strictly non-negative adjacency and +-1.27025 with rows balanced to zero. If SW_0156's
   inference-time variant shows no effect, that measurement says where to look — their `A0`
   still supplies alpha, cost and pulse coupling, so the repulsion may be entering a
   narrower channel than it appears.
3. **`PV2_0087`**: Slot Attention's forward pass does not depend on the evaluation grid, so
   a Slot64 column is the same computation as a Slot32 one scored differently, while a
   native64 model of our shared family costs roughly 18.6x its native16 self per 4x grid
   refinement. Relevant to SW_0154's resource framing.
